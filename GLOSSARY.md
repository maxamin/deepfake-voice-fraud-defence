# Glossary

- **Deepfake:** synthetic or manipulated audio, image or video that imitates a real person.
- **Voice clone:** a speech synthesis or conversion model that imitates a specific speaker.
- **Call-back verification:** confirming a request by calling a number from an independent, pre-existing record.
- **Business email compromise (BEC):** fraud that impersonates executives or suppliers to redirect payments.
- **Presentation attack:** showing an artefact (photo, screen, mask) to a biometric sensor.
- **Injection attack:** feeding synthetic or replayed media into a capture pipeline without passing the physical sensor.
- **PAD:** presentation attack detection.
- **APCER / BPCER:** rates of attack presentations wrongly accepted and bona fide presentations wrongly rejected.
- **Liveness:** checks that a live person is present during biometric capture.
- **Passive authentication:** verifying the issuer's signature over data stored on an identity document's chip.
- **EER (equal error rate):** the operating point where false-acceptance and false-rejection rates are equal.
- **C2PA / Content Credentials:** an open standard for signed provenance manifests attached to media.
- **Watermark:** a signal embedded in content to indicate origin, which may survive re-encoding.
