# Resources

## Tools

- [c2patool and the C2PA Rust SDK](https://github.com/contentauth/c2pa-rs) — inspect and sign content credentials.
- [Content Credentials verify](https://contentcredentials.org/verify) — web verification of C2PA manifests.
- [AASIST](https://github.com/clovaai/aasist) — open audio anti-spoofing baseline.
- [AudioSeal](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audioseal) — localised audio watermarking.
- [FaceForensics++](https://github.com/ondyari/FaceForensics) — video manipulation detection benchmark.
- [mitmproxy](https://mitmproxy.org/), [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) — test tooling for replay and virtual-camera checks in authorised environments.
- [Google Play Integrity API](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity), [Apple App Attest](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/devicecheck) — device and app integrity.

## Datasets and challenges

- [ASVspoof challenges](https://www.asvspoof.org/).
- Müller et al., "Does Audio Deepfake Detection Generalize?" (In-the-Wild dataset), Interspeech 2022.
- Dolhansky et al., "The DeepFake Detection Challenge (DFDC) Dataset", 2020.

## Papers and standards

- Jung et al., "AASIST", ICASSP 2022.
- Rössler et al., "FaceForensics++", ICCV 2019.
- San Roman et al., "Proactive Detection of Voice Cloning with Localized Watermarking", ICML 2024.
- ISO/IEC 30107-3, presentation attack detection testing and reporting.
- CEN/TS 18099 (2024), biometric data injection attack detection.
- [NIST SP 800-63 Digital Identity Guidelines](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/).
- [ICAO Doc 9303](https://www.icao.int/publications/doc-series/doc-9303).
- [C2PA specifications](https://c2pa.org/specifications/).

## Advisories and reporting

- FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov/) — public service announcements and reporting.
- FinCEN alert on deepfake media fraud schemes (November 2024), [fincen.gov](https://www.fincen.gov/).
- FCC declaratory ruling on AI-generated voices in robocalls (February 2024), [fcc.gov](https://www.fcc.gov/).
- UK [Action Fraud](https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/) and [NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/).
